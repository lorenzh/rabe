# View behavior

Cron facts omit a readable schedule that repeats the raw expression. Workflow facts and the cost panel use `1 agent` for one child and `agents` for all other counts. Ended shells, Codex jobs and workflows keep their stop controls dim with their hotkeys bound to `NONE`. Pressing them sends no stop request, toast or action note.

**Items tab.** The search button caps its query at 16 cells with `…`, or fewer when the button cannot fit. The Input keeps the full query at every width. Groups: failed first, then agents (Claude, Codex, workflows), shells, monitors, cron; an item found while the order is held goes into the group of its kind (see [The held order](focus-order.md#the-held-order)); inside a group running items first, then the newest (as held). A Codex job follows the agent that started it, after that agent's other jobs (`nest`), and is indented two cells under it, or under that agent's job before it. While the order is held a job keeps its held place, and a job found since joins its agent's block like a new shell (see [Rows found later in a list](focus-order.md#rows-found-later-in-a-list)). An agent that only forwarded to the Codex companion (`isForwarder`: it made at least one companion call, every tool call it made was one (`toolCount` equals the calls; an agent from before Rabe loaded has no count and stays), and a job is linked to it) has no row: `shown(items)` leaves it out of the Items, Cost and Timeline lists, the held order and the band's count, and its job stands for both. While the pane is open a row that folds leaves the list (see [Rows found later in a list](focus-order.md#rows-found-later-in-a-list)). A group header is `▾ AGENTS`: the arrow in the group's color, the name a Button that folds the group (`▸` when folded), and a dim note (`2 claude · 1 codex`, `2 running`). A row reads marker, glyph (colored by `tone`), the name as its Button, a shell's port and exit code, and the time, or the status word and age once it ended, at the right end. The selected row has an orange `▌`, the `selected` background and a plain time; other names and times are dim.

`byParent(list, items)` in `lists.ts` groups shells and monitors by who started them: the main session's first (title `''`, drawn without a header), then one block per agent in the order of its first item in `list` with the agent item (for its glyph) and its title, `run › agent` for a workflow agent and `agent n/a` for an agent Rabe no longer holds. In SHELLS and MONITORS the Items tab draws each agent's block under a header that is Text, not a row: the agent's glyph in its `tone` (a dim `◐` for `agent n/a`) and its title in dim. The children's rows are indented two cells after the marker. The list order, the first row that takes the focus and `rowKeys` follow the drawn order, so the arrows walk the children where they stand. The blocks are cut from the held order. A shell that came after the open joins its agent's block, after the block's held rows; the pane moves the ring back when that puts a row above the focused one (see [Rows found later in a list](focus-order.md#rows-found-later-in-a-list)). A failed shell stays in FAILED without its agent. The band counts the shells and monitors of agents with the others.

**Detail per kind.** The detail has three parts. `detailView` draws them in full. `detailLines(model, item, rows, width, sel?)` builds the split preview (`summary` in `views/items.ts`). The head (`headLines`) is the glyph, the title, the status word on the right, and the fact lines in dim. The top precedes the body: a cost panel and the brief or prompt, or the label of the body (`▸ output`, `▸ next runs`). The full detail keeps every body line (`bodyLines`). Commands and cron prompts use `Facts.long` too. Both details keep their full text. CR, LF and CRLF breaks show `↵` before a new row. Wrapping preserves spaces and uses no ellipsis. Only the split preview cuts older body lines and marks hidden text:

- Agent: the facts add `agent <id>` and, last, `transcript <path>` (`n/a` without one) in full: `Facts.long` is cut into lines of the detail's width (`headLines(model, item, columns)`), never with `…`, so the agent file's name at the end of a long path shows, and `c: copy id` copies the agent id (in the running session `m` messages and resumes an agent). A cost panel (background `C.panel`): `≈ $0.16   in 36k  out 5k  cached 12k`, then `45% of session` and, while it runs, `active 3s ago` from `lastToolAt`. Then `▸ brief` with the prompt the agent was given, or the description for an agent Rabe did not see spawn (at most three lines, cut with `…`), then the turns from `rabe.turns`: `1  ● text` (index dim, `●` orange), then `⎿ Tool summary` per tool. Turns with tools and no text show only their tool lines. The last turn of a running agent has the raised background `C.raised`. An agent that ran before Rabe loaded has no turns and says so.
- Codex: the facts say model, effort and sandbox, the job id, the thread id (`n/a` until the job file has one) and whether the session file was read, is partial (an unread backlog or a skipped oversized record) or is gone. The incremental reader continues through files over 4 MiB. The detail keeps the last 50 steps. With a thread id, `c: copy resume` copies `codex resume <thread id>`. A job folded with its forwarder says `forwarded by claude <title> · its tokens count here` instead of `started by`, its cost panel adds the forwarder's tokens and dollars (`withForwarder`; tokens, cached tokens or dollars that either side lacks make the sum unknown, never the other side's part), and `f: open forwarder` (key `forwarder:<agent item id>`) opens the forwarder's own detail with its turns, id and transcript. The forwarder has no row, so `b: back` from it selects its job's row (`rowOf` in `lists.ts`). The cost panel adds the command count and takes `active` from `sessionUpdatedAt`. Then `▸ prompt`, then `detail.steps`: `●` (cyan) messages, `thinking:` reasoning summaries, and `$ command` with `✓ exit 0 · N lines`, `✗ exit N` or `◐ running` on the right; the running command is raised. `x: stop` cancels the job through `/rabe-stop` (see [Stopping an item](sources.md#stopping-an-item)).
- Workflow: the cost panel sums the run's agents and counts them. Then the phases as `✓ Review → ◐ Verify → · Report`, and per phase its agents in start order with their tokens and time (`22k · 40s`). The start order is held like the Timeline's (`stable` with `rabe.order.timeline`), so an agent that ends never moves under the focus. Workflow agents use the workflow's transcript and meta directory even when Claude Code gives them a generic transcript path. Rabe keeps checking completed agents whose phase metadata is not ready while the run declares phases. In the full detail each agent row is a plain Button that opens it, `b: back` keeps the one `autoFocus`, and the arrow keys walk the agent rows like a list: the row that holds the focus is selected and marked (`▌`, the `selected` background), the others are dim. The full detail draws every agent row, not the newest that fit; the pane scrolls.
- Shell: `▸ output · newest last · N lines` (`1 line` for one) (N is `seen`, all lines read), the lines in `rabe.lines`, and at the end `✓ exit 0` in green or `✗ exit N` in red once the exit code is known.
- Monitor: `▸ lines received · newest last`, each line after the time the poll first read it.
- Cron: the facts show `last fired <time> · <count> fires` for confirmed transcript pairs. An observed turn adds `turn ended answered`, `stopped`, `error` or `refused`. Ambiguous matches show no fire details. These words describe the turn end, not job success. Then `▸ next runs`, the next five times from the schedule using the pane duration format (`10:55  in 3m00s`); a `/loop` wakeup shows `▸ fires` and its one time. Only running items show future times. Delete shows for cron jobs only: a wakeup has no id `CronDelete` knows. An ended job keeps its delete control in place, dim with its hotkey bound and no action.

Cost, tokens and the share of the session left the fact lines for the panel; the spend there is the same sum the Cost tab uses. Cron runs seen this session and the per-tool exit codes of agents are not shown: no source records them.

**Cost tab** (`views/cost.ts`). A head row on a panel: the session's cost (`Model.usd`, from `$.session.usage().cost`, as `/cost` totals it; `session cost n/a` without it), the Claude and Codex estimates (`totals`, shown only when that source exists and `n/a` when a worker has no estimate), tokens, the running count and the number of workers without tokens; under it `session <id>` (`Model.sessionId`, from `$.session.id()`), with `c: copy resume` in the toolbar, which copies `claude --resume <id>`. Then a row per agent and Codex job (a folded forwarder's tokens and dollars in its job's row, `n/a` when either side lacks them), sorted by tokens when the pane opened (held): marker, glyph, the name as a Button that opens the worker in the Items tab, a bar in eighths of a block scaled to the largest, tokens, cost and run time. The name gets up to 36 columns when the pane allows it. The bar takes the kind's chip color while the worker runs, green or red once it ended, and `▏` in dim where tokens are `n/a`. The header says "sorted by tokens when opened" when an opening order is held. New workers follow the held rows. Without a held order, the header says "sorted by tokens". Under **LOAD**, running agents that look slow, from `rabe.turns`: `◷ long tool` when the last step asked for tools 2 minutes ago or more (the tool still runs), `⚠ stuck` when the last step asked for none and no step came for 5 minutes. The foot names where the session cost comes from and says when a Codex session file is gone.

The Cost tab groups its rows by **agent** (above, with the main session's row sorted by tokens among the workers; a held order puts it last, and the tokens total counts it), **model** or **harness** (`sel.grouping` from `rabe.grouping`; the buttons `i: agent`, `o: model`, `h: harness`, keys `by-agent`, `by-model`, `by-harness`; one is active at a time and the header says `by agent`, `by model` or `by harness`). The model and harness rows come from `groupRows` in `ui/groups.ts`: each worker counts once, a forwarder's spend inside its Codex job as in the agent grouping (`withForwarder`), so Claude rows equal the band and Codex is shown on top. An agent's model is its last `detail.model` (a switch inside one agent is not split); a worker without a model is in `model n/a`. A row's cost is `n/a` when one of its workers has none, as in the totals. A row's tokens are `n/a` when no worker has a count; when only some have one, the total leaves the others out and the row's name ends in `(partial)`. Group rows are `row:by:<grouping>:<key>` Buttons whose press only keeps the focus; a row id of one grouping is not found in another, so the focus falls to the first row. They hold their order like the worker rows (`rabe.order` keys `cost:model` and `cost:harness`, taken at open). The last column shows the worker count (`WORKERS`).

The **main session** is not an item and has its own rows in every grouping, labelled `main session` with the word `(partial)` after the name, which a narrow pane never cuts: per model in the model grouping, one row in the others. Its tokens are the steps with no `agentId` that Rabe saw since it loaded (`rabe.mainUsage`), so a session resumed or started before the mod loaded is incomplete; hence the label. Its dollars are an estimate from those steps at the 1-hour cache-write rate (`cache_write_1h`; the step usage has no 5m and 1h split, and the main loop writes 1-hour entries), `n/a` when a model has no price. They are not subtracted from the session cost: whether `$.session.usage().cost` includes subagents is not verified ([feasibility](../feasibility/files.md)), so Rabe shows no main-session remainder.

**Effects tab** (`views/effects.ts`). `touched(items, edits, cwd, worktrees)` in `touched.ts` collects the changed files of three kinds of editor (`Editor`): each agent's `detail.edits` (the `Edit` and `Write` calls that changed a file, not the calls a turn asks for, which may be refused or staged, and its shell writes from host diffs or disk checks), each Codex job's `detail.edits`, and the main session's `rabe.edits` (`Model.edits`, editor `main session`). With git's worktrees (`Model.worktrees`, from `rabe.worktrees`) each file belongs to the worktree whose path is the longest prefix of its path (`treeOf`; paths compare through `isInside` and `pathKey` in `model.ts`, so a Windows path matches whatever its slashes and letter case, and one file under two spellings is one row), whoever changed it, the main session included, and is shown relative to that worktree. Without them, or for a file outside every worktree, a path is shown relative to the editor's worktree, `cwd`, Codex `workspaceRoot` or the session's `cwd` (`Model.cwd`). A long path loses its start, so the file name stays. A file two editors changed under one absolute path is a conflict (`isConflict`), also the main session and an agent: the tab heads with `⚠ conflict  src/logger.ts is edited by A and B in the main tree` on a red background (`in <worktree>` names a tree only when it is proven to hold the file: the worktree git places it in, `the main tree` for git's main worktree, or the worktree of an editor (Claude Code's metadata) whose path contains it; else the banner names no place), and lists conflicts first, then the newest changes (`byConflict`, held while the pane is open). A relative path is never a conflict; no source records one today, so this only guards against a stray tool result. Each row says who changed the file and how: the count when more than one (`3×`), then `edit`, `write`, `via shell` (host diff or disk check) or `codex add`, `codex update`, `codex delete`, and a red `deleted · ` when the last change deleted it. Every source records absolute paths only (a shell write's relative path is skipped only when its folder is unknown, see feasibility), so the row has no mark for an unknown folder. Each file row is a plain Button on the file name that opens the agent or Codex job that changed it last, or copies the path when that was the main session; the selected one has the `▌` marker and the focus. Two worktrees never share an absolute path, so the same file in two worktrees is no conflict. Then WORKTREES (`worktreeRows`), one row per worktree in use, `⎇ name  branch · who`, and the header counts these rows. With git a worktree is in use when a file in it changed (its row names the editors, the main session included) or an agent runs in it: its `worktreePath` when git lists that path, else its `cwd`, unless it changed a file in some worktree, which already places it. The main worktree reads `main tree`. An agent worktree git does not list keeps its row from the agent's metadata, after git's. An agent in a subfolder of a worktree (its `cwd` only) sits in that worktree's row, never in a row of its own. Without git the rows come from agent metadata as before: each agent worktree, then `main tree` for the agents whose `cwd` is the session's and that have no worktree. Last, `n/a` counts the agents whose tree is not known (no `cwd`, with git a `cwd` outside every worktree, without git a `cwd` that is not the session's). Then the ports of running shells, each with its `ssh -L` line as a plain Button that copies it. A port has one row however many shells report it (`portsOf`), since two rows with one key would trap the arrow keys on the first; the row names the first shell that has it. While the order is held, FILES and PORTS show the rows of the open in held order, then the files first edited and the ports found since, in the order Rabe found them. A port whose shell ended leaves the view, also while the pane is open (see [Rows found later in a list](focus-order.md#rows-found-later-in-a-list)). The first port's line has a background and the hotkey `c`, which the engine draws as `c: ssh -L …`. The tab has no action buttons in its toolbar. The first port row owns `c` while that port exists. Every file is a row and the tab scrolls: a file list cut to the rows the other sections leave would gain or lose Buttons above the `ssh -L` lines whenever a port, a worktree or a conflict came or went.

**Timeline tab** (`views/timeline.ts`). The tab shows a window of time, `sel.window` (`RabeWindow`). `/rabe` sets it to the last `timelineHours` hours (the `userConfig` option, default 4; `hoursOf` reads it, `0` is no limit, anything that is not a number of hours is 4) and writes it to `rabe.window`. Its start `since` stays put while the pane is open, so the window grows with the time and no row folds away under the focus. Items that ended before `since` fold into one line of plain Text, `+37 older items, ended before 10:20`, above the rows: not a row, so it holds no Button and the focus order of the rows stays. Running and idle items and items that ended inside the window stay rows; a bar that starts before the window is cut at the left edge and starts with `◂`. Who started what lists the same items. The control `w` (key `window`, before `c: copy resume` in the toolbar; neither is a stop) steps the window for the open pane, `widen`: base hours, 12 hours (when the base is less), the whole session, then base again; a wider step never starts later than the one before. Its label names the next step (`w: show 12 h`, `w: show all`). A step is the person's change of the view, so the hold starts anew, the pane disarms, and `act` lands the ring on `tab-timeline`, then `window`. With the option at `0` the window is the whole session. There is no `w` control. That letter reaches the prompt. The Items and Cost tabs show every item either way. Then a head with the window (`last 40 min`, `last 4h05m` from two hours on), a legend of chip colors and a time axis of round clock times and `now`: at most four labels, spaced 1, 2, 5, 10, 15 or 30 minutes or 1 to 24 hours apart as the window needs, each placed where its time falls (`10:20  10:30  10:40  now`). A label that would repeat the one before it or run into it or into `now` is left out, so a short window never names one minute twice. When no round clock label fits in a short window and space permits, the axis shows its computed range start. This needs no state write. One row per item in the window, sorted by start when the pane opened (held): the name as a Button that opens it, then a bar from its start to its end (or now) in the kind's chip color while it runs, green or red once it ended. A cron job draws a tick at each scheduled run since Rabe saw it and before it ended, computed with `nextRun`. The legend says "scheduled run". These ticks remain forecasts. Confirmed actual fire times appear in cron detail only. A space parts the name, cut with `…`, from its bar. Below: who started what (`tree`, with the kind in brackets for all but Claude agents; a forwarder stays in it, with its job under it), and the previous session in this project (`Model.previous`): side by side from 90 columns, else one under the other. The previous session ends with ` id <session id>`, `n/a` for a summary without one; with an id, `c: copy resume` in the toolbar copies `claude --resume <id>`.



### Removing ended items

The list keeps every item until the cap drops it (200 ended items), so a long session fills it with finished work. `r: remove` (key `remove`, action `remove` with the selected item) hides the selected row once it ended; `a: remove ended` (key `clear`, action `clear`) hides every ended row the search shows. Both are drawn always, dim while they cannot act (a running or no selected row; no ended row), and nothing running is ever removed.

- **State.** `remove($, action)` in `pane.tsx` adds the ids to `rabe.removed` with the versioned retry of the write loop (`ifVersion`; two removes in flight read the same value, and a plain write would bring one row back) and toasts what went ("Removed shell bg_3", "Removed 4 ended items"). For `clear` it takes the ended items at the press, from `rabe.items` and the search in `rabe.query`. The items stay in `rabe.items`: a poll that finds an ended item again (a task output file, `CronList`) adds or merges it in the registry, and its id still hides it, so nothing comes back. The pane and the band pass every item and `rabe.removed` (`Model.removed`) to the views, and only list rows go: the Items list with its empty note and the count on its tab, and the band's chips, leave the removed items out through `kept(items, removed)`, so the arrow keys and Enter on the list never reach a removed row. Every other view keeps them, so removing never makes history false: a workflow's agent rows and its phases with their counts and tokens, the Cost tab's rows and totals (the rows add up to the totals), the Timeline bars, the cost line in the band, the Effects tab, and the order `/rabe` takes (#206). An id whose item is running again (an agent that resumed) shows again, since `kept` never hides a running item. An id stays after the cap drops its item, since a poll may find that item again; the value is session state, so it survives a reload and starts empty in a new session. The previous-session summary counts every item, removed or not, except cron jobs that ended before the session.
- **Focus.** A removed row leaves the Items list at once, also while the pane is open: the hold drops a gone row below the toolbar and holds row presses back while the ring may sit on another row (see [Rows found later in a list](focus-order.md#rows-found-later-in-a-list)). An open item and the Cost and Timeline tabs keep the row. When it was the selected row, the list falls back and `x`, `g` and `r` go inert until the person moves onto a live row (see [Arming](focus-order.md#arming)).
- **Arming.** Both keys are destructive: they act only while the pane is armed. `r` acts on the selection like `x`, so it also needs `isListArmed`, and it is in `LIST_KEYS`: a target it gains without the person (the selected row ended, the list fell back) disarms, as for `x` and `g`. `a` acts on no selection but on "every ended row", the same target whatever ends meanwhile, so it needs only `isArmed`, and its key keeps its target in the walk of `render.test.tsx`.



### The previous session

On `session.end` (`{ reason: /^/ }`) `pane.tsx` keeps a summary of the session in `$.store` under `previous:<cwd>`: `previousOf(items, now, usage, sessionId)` counts the items per kind (cron jobs still running only) and holds the tokens, the session's cost and start, the ids of failed items (`failedIds`) and the id `claude --resume` takes (`e.resume.id`, else `e.sessionId`) (`RabePrevious` in `types/index.d.ts`). `sessionId` is optional: a summary of Rabe 0.3 or older has none and shows `n/a`, and a value that is not a string is dropped when read. The Timeline tab names the scope of each figure, since they differ: `session ≈ $0.32 · 1h12m` is the whole session's cost (`cost n/a` when unknown) and duration, main session included, and `background 800k tok` is the tokens of the background items only. On read, Rabe removes older records’ `failed` titles and saves empty `failedIds`. An empty session also cleans its previous record. The other summary fields stay. A session without items keeps the last summary, so a short session with no background work does not hide the one before. The pane reads the key on each draw; a value of another shape shows as no previous session. After `/clear` the summary is the cleared conversation's.


**Live updates.** `session.start` starts `$.clock.every(1000)`, which calls `$.ui.invalidate('ui.render')` while any item runs.

**Actions.** Stop calls `TaskStop` with the task id (shells, monitors, workflows) or the agent id, and runs `/rabe-stop <item id>` for a Codex job. Delete calls `CronDelete` only while the current job is still running. Refusals show plain text without the tool error tags. Remove and remove ended write `rabe.removed` (see [Removing ended items](views.md#removing-ended-items)). Message calls `$.session.send` to the agent. Copy calls `$.ui.copy` on the surface that was pressed. Each answers with a toast: "Stopping …", "Stop refused: …", "Stopped 7 of 9; 2 had already finished", "Message sent to …". Stop, remove and message toasts use kind and native id. Every toast goes through `toast($, text)`. It applies `toastText` to refusal reasons from files and tools (see [security guidelines](../security.md)). A cron job's title is its prompt. Its toasts name the job id (`Deleted cron c1`). A copied prompt toast names the field and counts its lines and characters without the text.

Successful stop, group stop, cron delete and agent message actions append a note to `rabe.actions`, capped at 20. The matched `prompt.submit` hook adds these notes to the prompt's private `context`, preserving existing context, then removes the delivered prefix after the prompt enters. A refused or already-ended action adds no note. Notes contain only the item kind and id (`Rabe: user stopped shell shell:b1`): titles come from files or are prompts, so a note never carries one, and agent message text is never stored or sent in the note. The conditional state write preserves actions queued while a prompt is entering.

Each copy action declares its field type in `what`. `pane.tsx` refuses CR or LF for every field except commands and prompts. `clipText` keeps tabs and line breaks. It replaces every other control (`Cc`) and format (`Cf`) character with `?` before `$.ui.copy`.

Success toasts name the field and count the filtered clipboard text: `Copied command · 3 lines · 412 chars`. CRLF counts as one line break. Character counts use Unicode code points. Success toasts contain no copied text. Failure toasts use `Copy failed: <reason>.` with the API reason or caught error. If `toastText` leaves the original text unchanged, the toast offers `Select it: <text>`. Cron prompts never appear in toasts.

Before a view builds a fixed resume command, `validId` checks its id against `[A-Za-z0-9_][A-Za-z0-9_.-]*`. The Cost tab checks the current session id. The Timeline tab checks the previous session id. The Codex detail checks the thread id. Agent ids use the same check before copy. Invalid ids show `n/a` with no copy button. See the [clipboard rules](../security.md#terminal-output-and-the-clipboard) for the limits of copied commands.



### Hiding Claude Code's own count

Claude Code shows background work itself: a pill in the hint under the prompt (`2 shells, 1 monitor · ↓ to manage`) and a part of the line that ends a turn (`· 2 shells, 1 monitor still running`). Rabe's band shows the same, so `ui/builtin.tsx` hides both. It is on by default; the `userConfig` option `hideBuiltinTasks` in `.claude-plugin/plugin.json` turns it off, and `register` then does not call `builtin(on)`.

- `PromptHint`: `stripTasks(hint)` splits the hint at ` · ` and drops the parts that only count work (`2 shells, 1 monitor`, `1 background agent`) and `↓ to manage`. The hook passes the rest on with `next({ ...e, props: { ...e.props, hint } })`, so the PR number, `esc to interrupt` and the other parts stay. Rewriting the whole hint would drop them. The mode label (`⏵⏵ auto mode on`) is not part of `hint` and stays. ↓ still opens Claude Code's background manager.
- `TurnDuration`: the "still running" part is not a prop, so the hook draws the line itself (`✻ Brewed for 5s`, dim, one empty line above) while Rabe knows of a running shell or monitor and of no running agent or workflow. While an agent or workflow runs, the engine draws "Waiting for …" without that part, so the hook passes. The own line leaves out what has no prop: `done 1:16`, a token budget and "messages hidden".
- The agent list under the prompt stays: no component and no setting hides it (see [feasibility](../feasibility.md)).

Both views are tested on the `terminal` and `desktop` surfaces. Tests feed the session values with test hooks on `state.get`, fix the time with `mock.clock`, and read a drawing with `screen(ui)` from `fixtures.ts`: each row Box as one line, a Raster decoded, each control alone. The views can also be called directly with a model, since they are plain functions: `gridOf(drawn)` lays a view's lines into a grid as the terminal does, so `views/*.test.ts` check the text and the colors at positions. The test kit cannot answer a plugin's `$.ui.focus` (the call fails, as a refused one does), so the pane's tests check that the arrow hook keeps a one-row scroll and passes the rest, that a change of the view leaves the stops disarmed, that a person's `ui.focus` (raised by the test) arms them, that a plugin loaded beneath Rabe (`plugins` of the test, `append` tier) that sends the move onto another row, refuses it or swallows it leaves the selection on where the ring is and `x` and `g` inert, and that a press on another row selects it and arms `x` for it while a press on the selected row opens it; `view.test.ts` checks `stepRow` and `arm`, also the events the arrow hook feeds (`step`, the plugin's focus, `landed`), since the kit runs no hook for the plugin's own `$.ui.focus`.

### Idle teammates

Items shows `idle` beside the agent and in its group count. Before the pane opens, running and idle items sort together before ended items. Detail facts say `idle` and keep the active or last-step label. Idle agents count as neither running nor ended. Remove and Remove ended leave them visible. Their message and stop controls remain available with the same arming rules.

The band excludes idle agents from work counts and adds no idle chip. Cost shows `idle` before the worker name and never flags an idle agent stuck. Timeline shows `idle` before the name with a dim bar. The bar covers the agent's lifetime through now. Rabe does not store idle gaps or subtract them from elapsed time.

The window filter uses `!isEnded(item)` explicitly. The previous filter kept idle rows only because idle items have no `endedAt`.

Status changes keep the held list order and the append-only focus slots. New rows still append under the existing rules in [Focus order and arming](focus-order.md).
